Obsidian Harvest Design System

Built on a foundation of High-Contrast Minimalism. It prioritizes extreme legibility and functional speed for the food delivery experience. By moving away from trendy translucent effects, the system leans into Solid-State UI—where every element feels intentional, grounded, and permanent.

token

Brand Philosophy & Solid-State UI

layers

Tonal Layering

Intentionally avoids ambient shadows and blurs. Depth is communicated through strictly curated surface levels (#131313 base, #201f1f surface, #2D2D2D overlay).

square

Razor-Sharp Borders

Every surface element features a crisp 1px solid border. In this system, borders do the heavy lifting that shadows typically perform in other designs.

ads_click

Singular Accent Action

Electric Amber (#FF9F0D) is reserved exclusively for interactive calls-to-action, status indicators, and critical brand highlights.
